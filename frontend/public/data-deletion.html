User Data Deletion Instructions

Focalyt CRM Lead Integration

How to request deletion of personal data collected through Facebook and Instagram Lead Ads and stored in Focalyt CRM

📖

Overview

This page explains how you can ask Focal Skill Development Pvt. Ltd. (Focalyt) to delete personal data that the Meta application Focalyt CRM Lead Integration stored in Focalyt CRM after you submitted a Facebook or Instagram Lead Ad form. Deletion requests are handled by email to info@focalyt.com. There is no in-app or self-service delete option, and opening this page does not delete a record.

📊 1. Data This Request Can Cover

📱 Meta Lead Ads

Personal information Focalyt CRM Lead Integration stores from a connected Lead Ad form:

  • Name
  • Mobile number
  • Email address, when the form included one
  • WhatsApp number, or the mobile number when the form had no separate WhatsApp answer
  • Gender, only when the answer was male or female
  • Lead ID, Page ID, Form ID, and, when Meta sent them, Ad ID and submission time

🗂️ Focalyt CRM

Matching records in Focalyt CRM created from that lead, which may include:

  • Contact details and inquiry information
  • Follow-up notes and communication history
  • Course or program interest recorded by the institute
  • Source information showing the lead came from Meta Lead Ads

✉️ 2. How to Request Deletion by Email

To request deletion of your personal data, send an email to info@focalyt.com with the subject line User Data Deletion Request.

Please include as many of the following details as you can so we can locate the correct records:

Needed to find the record

  • Full name
  • Mobile number used on the lead form
  • A clear statement that you want your personal data deleted
  • Email address, if the form included one

Helpful if you have them

  • Approximate date you submitted the Meta Lead Ad form
  • Institute, course, or campaign name
  • Facebook or Instagram profile name used when submitting the form

You may copy this template:

To: info@focalyt.com

Subject: User Data Deletion Request

I request deletion of my personal data collected through Focalyt CRM Lead Integration and stored in Focalyt CRM. My name is [your name], mobile [your mobile], email [your email, if you provided one]. I submitted a Facebook or Instagram lead form on or around [date], for [institute, course, or job if known].

🛠️ 3. How Focalyt Processes Your Request

No self-service deletion. Focalyt CRM does not currently provide a “Delete Lead” button for users or institutes. We do not automatically delete existing leads when this page is visited, when you submit a Meta form, or when you send an email.
  1. We acknowledge your email and verify that we can identify the matching records.
  2. A Focalyt team member reviews the Focalyt CRM record and the stored Lead Ads identifiers associated with the details you provided. The educational institute does not complete this request through a self-service control.
  3. If the request is verified, Focalyt manually removes or de-identifies the matching personal data held in Focalyt CRM. There is no automatic deletion.
  4. We reply by email to confirm the outcome of the request.

⏱️ 4. Timeframe and Scope

Response time

We aim to acknowledge your request within 7 days and complete a verified request within 30 days of receiving the information we need to locate your records.

What this does not change automatically

Deleting Focalyt CRM information does not delete information Meta stores independently. To manage data held by Meta, use Facebook or Instagram settings and Meta’s own tools in addition to contacting Focalyt.

We may retain limited information if we are required to do so by law (for example, to keep a record that a deletion request was received and processed). We will tell you if we cannot locate a matching record.

🔐 5. Identity Verification

To protect other people’s data, we only process a deletion request after we can reasonably match it to records we hold. We may ask you to confirm a phone number or email already associated with the lead. We will not delete records based on incomplete or unmatched requests.
