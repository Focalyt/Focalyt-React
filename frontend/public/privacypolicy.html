1. Introduction

Welcome to Focalyt.

Focalyt is an educational management and CRM platform designed to help educational institutions manage student enquiries, admissions, courses, training programs, student records, and communications.

This Privacy Policy explains how Focalyt collects, processes, uses, stores, shares, and protects personal information when users interact with our website, educational management platform, CRM services, WhatsApp Business integrations, and Facebook and Instagram Lead Ads integrations.

This policy also applies to our Meta application, Focalyt CRM Lead Integration, which connects Facebook and Instagram Lead Ads with Focalyt CRM.

We respect user privacy and are committed to handling personal information responsibly, transparently, and in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

When you use our services or submit information through connected lead forms, your information may be processed as described in this policy, subject to applicable consent and other legal requirements.

2. Facebook and Instagram Lead Ads Integration

2.1 About Focalyt CRM Lead Integration

Focalyt CRM Lead Integration is an application that connects Facebook and Instagram Lead Ads with the Focalyt CRM platform.

Educational institutions can connect their authorized Facebook Pages and Lead Ads forms to Focalyt CRM to receive and manage enquiries generated through Facebook and Instagram advertisements.

When someone submits a connected Lead Ads form, the integration retrieves the information supplied in that form and creates or updates the corresponding enquiry record in Focalyt CRM.

The integration operates only for Facebook Pages and lead forms connected and authorized by the participating educational institution.

2.2 Information Collected from Meta Lead Ads

When a user submits a Facebook or Instagram Lead Ads form connected to Focalyt CRM, we may receive the following information:

  • Full name
  • Mobile or telephone number
  • Email address
  • WhatsApp contact number
  • Gender, if requested in the form
  • Answers to questions included in the Lead Ads form
  • Course, training program, or job preferences
  • Lead form submission date and time
  • Facebook Lead ID
  • Facebook Page ID
  • Lead Form ID
  • Advertisement ID
  • Other form information voluntarily provided by the user

The actual information collected depends on the fields included in the Lead Ads form configured by the educational institution.

We do not automatically collect information from unrelated Facebook Pages or Lead Ads forms that have not been connected to Focalyt CRM.

2.3 How We Receive and Process Meta Lead Data

When a user submits a connected Facebook or Instagram Lead Ads form:

  1. Meta sends a webhook notification indicating that a new lead has been submitted.
  2. The notification includes technical identifiers such as the Lead ID, Page ID, Form ID, and relevant submission details.
  3. Focalyt CRM Lead Integration uses the authorized Meta integration to retrieve the information submitted by the user.
  4. Focalyt identifies the educational institution and the course, job, or enquiry category associated with the connected form.
  5. The application processes and validates the submitted information.
  6. A CRM lead or candidate record is created or updated based on the information provided.
  7. Authorized institutional staff can access the enquiry and follow up with the user.

A name and valid mobile number are required for the existing lead-creation process. Other details, such as email address, WhatsApp number, and gender, are stored when provided.

If an existing candidate record matches the submitted mobile number, Focalyt may associate the new enquiry with that record instead of creating a duplicate person.

Technical lead identifiers may be retained to identify and process submissions and prevent duplicate processing.

2.4 Purpose of Using Meta Lead Data

Information received through Facebook and Instagram Lead Ads is used to:

  • Register user enquiries in Focalyt CRM.
  • Associate enquiries with the correct educational institution.
  • Identify courses, training programs, or jobs relevant to the enquiry.
  • Allow authorized institutional staff to contact the interested user.
  • Manage admission-related or program-related follow-ups.
  • Maintain enquiry history and communication records.
  • Prevent duplicate lead creation.
  • Support CRM operations and enquiry management.

We do not sell personal information collected through Meta Lead Ads.

Focalyt does not use this integration to create unrelated advertising audiences or market the data to unrelated third parties.

2.5 Access to Meta Lead Data

Personal information received through Focalyt CRM Lead Integration may be accessible to:

  • Authorized users of the educational institution that received the enquiry.
  • Focalyt personnel who require access for authorized technical support, security, or platform operations.
  • Service providers that support the secure operation of Focalyt CRM, subject to appropriate contractual and confidentiality protections.
  • Authorities when disclosure is required by applicable law.

Lead information is associated with the institution responsible for the connected advertisement.

Focalyt does not intentionally make one institution's lead records accessible to unrelated institutions.

2.6 Meta Account Permissions and Integration Control

Educational institutions must authorize the connection between their Facebook Pages and Focalyt CRM.

The application uses Meta permissions and technical access necessary to operate the authorized Lead Ads integration.

Institutions can disconnect their Facebook integration or manage permissions through the relevant Meta or Focalyt settings.

Disconnecting the integration prevents future retrieval through that connection but does not necessarily delete lead information previously stored in Focalyt CRM.

Requests to delete previously collected information are handled through the process described in Section 9.

5. Information Sharing and Disclosure

Focalyt does not sell personal information.

We may share or make information accessible only where necessary for legitimate service operations or where legally permitted.

5.1 Educational Institutions

Personal information submitted through a connected institution's lead form is made available to authorized staff of that institution for enquiry management and follow-up.

5.2 Authorized Staff and Educational Personnel

Relevant student and academic information may be accessible to administrators, instructors, or other authorized personnel as required to provide educational services.

5.3 Technology and Service Providers

We may use third-party technology providers for services such as cloud hosting, infrastructure, messaging, authentication, and platform operations.

Such providers should access or process personal information only to provide their authorized services and in accordance with applicable contractual and legal requirements.

5.4 Meta and WhatsApp Services

Facebook, Instagram, and WhatsApp integrations involve services provided by Meta Platforms.

Meta independently processes information according to its own privacy policies and applicable terms.

Focalyt's deletion of a CRM record does not automatically remove information independently held by Meta.

5.5 Legal Requirements

We may disclose information when required to comply with applicable law, legal proceedings, lawful regulatory requests, or valid government orders.

5.6 Restrictions on Information Sharing

We do not intentionally:

  • Sell personal information to third-party data brokers.
  • Share one institution's confidential student records with unrelated institutions.
  • Disclose academic records to unauthorized parties.
  • Use Meta Lead Ads information for unrelated commercial purposes.

6. Data Storage and Retention

Personal information is stored in systems used to operate Focalyt's educational management and CRM services.

Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the relevant service, maintain legitimate operational records, fulfill applicable contractual obligations, or comply with legal requirements.

Retention may depend on the type of information, the institution's requirements, and applicable laws.

Lead information collected through Facebook and Instagram may remain in Focalyt CRM while the receiving educational institution manages the enquiry, unless deletion is requested or the information is no longer required.

When information is no longer necessary, we take appropriate steps to delete, anonymize, or otherwise dispose of it, subject to applicable legal retention obligations.

Users may request deletion of their information using the procedure described in Section 9.

9. Data Deletion Instructions

Users may request the deletion of personal information collected or stored through Focalyt services, including data received through our Meta application, Focalyt CRM Lead Integration.

9.1 How to Request Deletion

Option 1 — Email Request

Send an email to info@focalyt.com with the subject Personal Data Deletion Request.

Include the following information where available:

  • Your name
  • The phone number or email address associated with the enquiry
  • The name of the educational institution, if known
  • A short description of the information you want deleted

You do not need to provide every listed detail if it was not originally collected. We may request additional information only when reasonably necessary to locate the relevant record and verify the request.

Option 2 — Data Deletion Instructions Page

Visit https://focalyt.com/data-deletion and follow the instructions provided to submit a deletion request.

9.2 How We Handle Deletion Requests

After receiving a deletion request:

  1. We review the request and identify the relevant personal information.
  2. We may verify the requester's identity to protect against unauthorized deletion.
  3. Where necessary, we coordinate with the educational institution responsible for the information.
  4. We delete or anonymize relevant information when required and permitted by applicable law.
  5. Where information must be retained for legal or regulatory reasons, we restrict its handling as appropriate.
  6. We communicate the outcome of the request through the available contact channel.

Deletion requests are currently handled manually.

9.3 Facebook and Instagram Data Deletion

Users who submitted information through Facebook or Instagram Lead Ads may request deletion of the corresponding information stored in Focalyt CRM.

Deleting information from Focalyt CRM does not automatically delete information stored separately by Meta.

Users may manage information held by Facebook and Instagram through their respective account privacy controls.

9.4 Disconnection of an Integration

An educational institution may disconnect its Meta integration.

Disconnection stops future data retrieval through that integration but does not automatically remove records previously collected.

Previously stored records can be addressed through Focalyt's deletion procedures.

10. Children's Privacy and Educational Data

We recognize that children's personal information requires appropriate protection.
