📜 7. Educational Data Specific Disclosures

FERPA

FERPA is a United States law that can apply to certain US educational institutions and their education records. This application does not contain a FERPA school-official agreement or FERPA-specific controls. A US institution that uses Focalyt remains responsible for deciding whether FERPA applies and for meeting its own obligations. Focalyt processes student and enquiry information for the educational, admissions, and follow-up purposes described in this policy.

Educational Purpose Limitation

All data processing is strictly limited to legitimate educational purposes including academic instruction, student support, institutional administration, and educational research.

Directory Information

This policy does not designate student information as directory information. An institute that is subject to FERPA is responsible for its own directory-information notices and opt-outs. Focalyt does not publish a directory of students from Lead Ads records.

📱 8. Focalyt CRM Lead Integration (Facebook and Instagram Lead Ads)

Our Meta application Focalyt CRM Lead Integration connects Facebook and Instagram Lead Ads to Focalyt CRM. It is used only when an educational institute connects its Facebook Page in Focalyt and maps a Lead Ad form to a course or job in Focalyt CRM.

The application follows this process:

  1. An educational institute connects its Facebook Page to Focalyt CRM.
  2. The institute maps its Lead Ads form to a course or job.
  3. A prospective candidate submits a Facebook or Instagram Lead Ad form.
  4. Meta sends Focalyt a webhook containing the Lead ID, Page ID, and Form ID. The Ad ID and submission time are stored when Meta includes them.
  5. Focalyt CRM Lead Integration retrieves that lead through an authorized Meta API. The retrieval includes the form’s field data.
  6. Focalyt CRM uses the institute’s form mapping to create a candidate and enquiry, or to attach the enquiry to an existing candidate with the same mobile number.
  7. Authorized staff of that institute use the record for enquiry management and follow-up.

A name and a 10-digit mobile number are required before a CRM record is saved. The same Meta lead is stored once so it is not imported again.

Information this integration stores

Focalyt CRM Lead Integration stores the following when Meta provides them and they can be used:

  • Lead ID, Page ID, and Form ID
  • Ad ID, when Meta includes it
  • Submission time, when Meta includes it
  • Name
  • Mobile number
  • Email address, when the form includes one
  • WhatsApp number, when the form includes one. If it does not, the mobile number is also saved as the WhatsApp contact number on the candidate record.
  • Gender, only when the form answer is male or female

How we process and store it in Focalyt CRM

  • Webhook identifiers are stored so the same Meta lead is processed once.
  • Focalyt reads the institute’s form mapping, including the lead source, status, and mapped course or job.
  • The record is stored as a course or job enquiry for that institute.
  • If a candidate with the same mobile number already exists, Focalyt attaches the new enquiry to that existing record.
  • Other answers on the Lead Ad form may be included in the data Meta returns. Those other answers are not saved as separate candidate fields.

How this data is used

Focalyt CRM Lead Integration uses this information so the mapped educational institute can manage the enquiry, provide counselling, carry out admissions-related follow-up, and communicate about the mapped course or job. Focalyt does not sell this personal information. It is not used to build advertising audiences for other organisations.

Who receives it and who can access it

Meta sends the lead to Focalyt CRM Lead Integration. Focal Skill Development Pvt. Ltd. processes and stores it in Focalyt CRM in order to operate the integration. Authorized Focalyt CRM users of the mapped educational institute can view that institute’s record. The Lead Ads retrieval and save process does not pass these personal details to another marketing service. Meta independently keeps its own copy under Meta’s Privacy Policy. Deleting the Focalyt CRM record does not delete Meta’s copy.

Data retention

Focalyt CRM Lead Integration does not have an automatic retention period or a scheduled deletion job for Lead Ads data. Lead identifiers and the related candidate or enquiry record remain in Focalyt CRM until they are removed.

Information is deleted when Focalyt completes a verified deletion request, as described below, or when an authorized user removes the record through ordinary use of Focalyt CRM. Visiting this Privacy Policy does not delete a lead.

No fixed retention period is configured for these records. Focalyt may keep limited information where the law requires it, including a record that a deletion request was received. A specific number of days is not stated here because the application does not define one.

How to request deletion

You may request deletion of personal data that Focalyt collected through Meta Lead Ads and stored in Focalyt CRM. Send an email to info@focalyt.com with the subject User Data Deletion Request, or follow the instructions at https://focalyt.com/data-deletion.

Include your name, the mobile number used on the form, and a clear statement that you want the personal data deleted. If the form included an email address, include that too. The institute, course, job, or approximate submission date helps us find the record.

Focalyt reviews the request at info@focalyt.com. There is no self-service delete control for the person who submitted the form, and sending the email does not delete the record by itself. After Focalyt can match the request to a record, Focalyt manually deletes or de-identifies the matching Focalyt CRM information. The educational institute uses the record for follow-up, but the published deletion path is a request to Focalyt, not an automated action by the institute.

Focalyt may ask you to confirm the mobile number or email already stored with the lead. Focalyt may retain limited information where the law requires it, including a record that the request was received. We will tell you if a matching record cannot be found.

Deleting information from Focalyt CRM does not delete information that Meta stores independently. Use Facebook or Instagram settings and Meta’s own tools for Meta’s copy. There is no fee to make this request.
